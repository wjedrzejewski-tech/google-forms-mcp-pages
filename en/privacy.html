Privacy policy

This information applies to a test Google Forms MCP integration used with a single Google account for RCIiTT ZUT purposes. Contact for this integration: rciittzut@gmail.com.

Deployment status: access is limited to one account and synthetic-data testing. Processing of real respondent data has not been approved in this pilot.

1. What information may be accessible?

  • Google account data needed for identification and access control, in particular email address and profile name (the email and profile scopes).
  • Google Form content and structure: titles, descriptions, questions, answer options and form identifiers (the forms.body scope).
  • Submitted form responses, if the user explicitly invokes the relevant read tool (the forms.responses.readonly scope). Such responses may contain personal data.

2. For what purpose is the data used?

Data is accessed only to perform the requested operations within the connected integration: checking the account, creating unpublished forms, reading their structure, editing eligible forms and reading selected responses. The integration does not use Google Forms data for advertising or sale.

3. Where does the data go and where may it be stored?

A request originates from ChatGPT, passes through the MCP server running on Cloudflare Workers and is executed via the Google Forms API. Read results, including responses, may be returned to ChatGPT and appear in the conversation. Google, Cloudflare and OpenAI may also process and retain data under their own policies, depending on the services used and account settings.

The authentication layer uses Cloudflare KV to store the technical data required to manage sessions and OAuth permissions, including access or refresh tokens. The integration does not maintain a separate database copy of form responses. The retention period for authentication data and the full deletion process require separate operator review; no unverified automatic deletion period is declared here.

This public information page may be hosted on GitHub Pages. The website does not allow Google sign-in, does not fetch forms or responses and does not contain MCP server code. GitHub may process technical visit data, including IP addresses, under its own policies.

4. Who has access?

Access to MCP tools requires authorization and is restricted by the server to the rciittzut@gmail.com account. Seeing the Google consent screen does not itself grant access to MCP. Technical service providers may process data only to the extent necessary to provide their services.

5. How can access be revoked or a question be raised?

The integration can be disconnected in ChatGPT settings and its Google access can be revoked at your Google account connections page. To submit a request about data controlled by the operator or to ask how it can be removed, contact rciittzut@gmail.com. Revoking authorization does not automatically remove earlier ChatGPT messages or data independently retained by other services.

6. Information updates

Before expanding the app beyond this limited pilot, this information must be reviewed for data scope, retention periods, operator identity and applicable data-protection duties. Updates will be published at this address.
